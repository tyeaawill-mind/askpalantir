Company Basics

What does Palantir actually do?
It builds software that integrates an organization's scattered data into one usable system for governments and large companies. Full answer: What Does Palantir Do?

What do the names Gotham, Foundry, AIP, and Apollo mean?
Gotham is Palantir's original government/defense platform; Foundry is the commercial equivalent; AIP layers AI on top of both; Apollo is the infrastructure that deploys and updates all three, even in disconnected environments. See our dedicated pages: Gotham, Foundry, AIP, Apollo.

What is the Palantir Ontology?
The structured data layer underneath Foundry and Gotham that maps an organization's data into real-world objects and relationships — Palantir calls it a kind of "digital twin." Full answer: What Is the Palantir Ontology?

Where does the name "Palantir" come from?
From J.R.R. Tolkien's "Lord of the Rings" — a palantír is a seeing-stone. Full answer: What Does "Palantir" Mean?

Who founded Palantir, and when?
Peter Thiel, Alex Karp, and a founding team, in 2003. Full answer: Who Founded Palantir?

Who are Palantir's biggest customers?
A mix of government/defense agencies (U.S. Army, ICE, UK NHS) and commercial enterprises (Airbus, Walmart, Rio Tinto). Full answer: Who Are Palantir's Biggest Customers? and the full government contracts list.

Controversies

Does Palantir work with ICE?
Yes, since 2011, including a 2025 ImmigrationOS contract — a relationship that draws real, documented criticism and an equally real company defense. Full answer, both sides sourced: Does Palantir Work With ICE?

What is Palantir's contract with the IRS?
Since 2018, the IRS has used Palantir software to analyze tax and financial records, with reported payments exceeding $130 million. Full answer: What Is Palantir's IRS Contract?

Does Palantir spy on Americans?
