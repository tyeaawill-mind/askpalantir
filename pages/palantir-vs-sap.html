Palantir vs. SAP: Rivals, or Something Else?

Not affiliated with Palantir Technologies Inc. This page is an independent explainer based on publicly available sources. Nothing here is financial advice.

Palantir and SAP get compared often, but as of 2026 the relationship has shifted from a straightforward rivalry into something closer to a partnership layered on top of genuine overlap.

The core difference

SAP is an enterprise resource planning (ERP) company — its systems, especially S/4HANA, serve as the authoritative transactional records for a company's finance, supply chain, manufacturing, and HR functions, used by tens of thousands of large enterprises worldwide. Palantir doesn't compete as an ERP system; instead, it positions itself as an operational intelligence layer that sits across SAP and non-SAP systems alike, using its Ontology to model how data and decisions connect across an organization, and then letting AI agents or human operators act on that model directly.

The 2025–2026 partnership

In May 2025, SAP and Palantir announced an engineering partnership aimed at modernizing legacy ERP systems and accelerating cloud adoption without disrupting ongoing operations — a priority in industries like government and critical infrastructure, where downtime carries real operational risk. That relationship deepened at SAP's Sapphire conference in May 2026, where SAP named Palantir a strategic partner alongside Anthropic, NVIDIA, AWS, and Google Cloud, and Palantir's AIP became available as an SAP-endorsed application specifically for ERP migration work — extracting data from SAP's older ECC systems, building a semantic model in Foundry, and generating validated datasets for migration to S/4HANA without requiring a disruptive parallel system.

Where the collaboration actually shows up

Beyond migration, reporting describes Foundry being used as a "control tower" that pulls real-time inventory data from SAP systems, correlates it with logistics and demand signals, and can trigger actions like reorders or shipment rerouting, with results written back into SAP rather than existing in a disconnected side system. On the finance side, AIP agents are described as handling account reconciliation and compliance verification directly on live SAP data, without needing to extract it from SAP's own governed environment first.

So are they competitors or not?

Industry analysis suggests the "SAP vs. Palantir" framing oversimplifies the relationship: SAP is strongest when AI needs to operate inside well-defined, already-modeled business workflows, while Palantir is strongest when a decision has to span multiple systems, data types, and organizational boundaries that no single ERP was built to unify. In practice, many large enterprises are described as needing both — SAP as the system of record, Palantir as the layer that reasons and acts across it and everything else the organization runs.

Sources consulted: SAPinsider, SAP Community, Medium (industry analysis pieces), PeerSpot vendor comparisons. This is a fast-moving partnership as of 2026 — details of scope and product availability may continue to change.
