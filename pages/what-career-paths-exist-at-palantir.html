What Career Paths Exist at Palantir Beyond Engineering?

Not affiliated with Palantir Technologies Inc. This page is an independent explainer based on publicly available sources. See our dedicated pages on Forward Deployed Engineers, interview questions, and salary data for more depth on the engineering side specifically.

Palantir's public reputation centers heavily on its distinctive Forward Deployed Engineer role, covered in detail on our dedicated FDE page — but the company's actual roster of open roles spans a much wider range than engineering alone.

The range of roles, by compensation data

Crowdsourced compensation data from Levels.fyi — the same source used on our salary page — shows Palantir postings and reported pay across a wide functional range: Administrative Assistant roles at the lower end of the reported compensation scale, up through Sales, Product Management, Data Science, Legal, and Information Technologist positions reporting some of the highest total compensation figures in the company's dataset. This range reflects an organization that, despite its engineering-heavy public image, staffs the full set of functions any large enterprise software company needs to operate.

Business and deployment-adjacent roles

Beyond the technical Forward Deployed Engineer track, Palantir has historically hired for adjacent business-facing roles — sometimes called Deployment Strategists or Business Development roles — that work alongside FDEs on the same customer engagements but focus more on relationship management, contract strategy, and understanding a customer's organizational needs, rather than writing the underlying code. These roles trade deep technical work for deeper customer and domain expertise.

Corporate and functional roles

Like any large public company, Palantir maintains standard corporate functions — finance, legal, people operations (HR), marketing, and sales — supporting the AIP Bootcamp process covered on our dedicated Bootcamp page and the broader commercial growth described on our revenue growth page. These roles are less publicly discussed than the engineering side but represent a real and necessary share of the company's roughly tens of thousands of roles.

Internship and early-career paths

Palantir has run structured internship programs feeding into several of its core tracks, including software engineering and forward-deployed roles, generally following a hiring process similar in structure — though often somewhat abbreviated — to the full-time process described on our interview questions and hiring-difficulty pages.

Where to actually find open roles

Palantir's own careers site is the primary source for current openings across all these functions, since specific roles, locations, and team names change frequently — this page describes the general landscape of role types rather than a current, exhaustive job listing.

Sources consulted: Levels.fyi's role-level compensation database, cross-referenced with the hiring-process sourcing already compiled on our FDE, interview-difficulty, and interview-questions pages. This page will be updated if Palantir's public role structure changes meaningfully.
