What Does Palantir Pay? A Look at Salaries and Compensation

Not affiliated with Palantir Technologies Inc. This page is an independent explainer based on publicly available, crowdsourced compensation data. Individual offers vary by role, level, location, and negotiation — treat these figures as a general reference, not a guarantee.

Palantir's pay is generally competitive with large tech companies, though reported figures vary meaningfully depending on the data source, role, and level — worth knowing before treating any single number as definitive.

Software Engineer compensation

Levels.fyi, which verifies submissions against real offer letters and W-2s, reports total compensation for Software Engineers at Palantir in the United States ranging from roughly $185,000 to $650,000 per year, with a median around $350,000 — split roughly evenly between base salary, annual stock grants, and bonus. Glassdoor's crowdsourced (self-reported, unverified) data reports a lower figure: an average base salary around $151,000–$153,000, with total pay closer to $207,000 once bonuses and additional compensation are included. The gap between the two sources is worth noting explicitly — Levels.fyi's verification process and its skew toward candidates negotiating offers likely pulls its figures higher than Glassdoor's broader, self-reported sample.

Forward Deployed Engineer compensation

For the Forward Deployed (Software) Engineer role — covered in more detail on our dedicated FDE explainer — Levels.fyi reports total compensation ranging from about $171,000 to $295,000, with a median around $207,500–$211,000, somewhat lower than the broader Software Engineer band above, which likely reflects a different mix of experience levels reporting data for this specific title.

Pay varies significantly by location

Levels.fyi's location-specific data shows real variation: the Greater Denver and Boulder area, for instance, shows a median total compensation around $240,000, below the national figures cited above — consistent with the general pattern that total compensation at most large tech employers skews higher in the highest-cost-of-living hubs.

Beyond engineering

Levels.fyi's broader salary database for Palantir shows a wide range across non-engineering roles too, from roughly $77,000 in total compensation for an Administrative Assistant role at the low end to over $400,000 for senior Information Technologist positions at the high end — see our page on career paths at Palantir beyond engineering for more on the range of roles available.

How pay has been described historically

Older reporting on Palantir's compensation (from 2019) described base pay as roughly in line with large peers like Apple, Microsoft, Google, and IBM, alongside notably generous perks that were later scaled back as the company matured. More recent data suggests total compensation, particularly the equity component, has grown substantially as Palantir's stock price has risen — a reminder that a meaningful share of reported "total compensation" at a public company is stock-based and will fluctuate with the share price rather than being fixed like a salary.

Sources consulted: Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Dice.com, DesignGurus.io. Compensation data is self-reported or crowdsourced and updates frequently — check each source directly for the most current figures before relying on them for negotiation.
